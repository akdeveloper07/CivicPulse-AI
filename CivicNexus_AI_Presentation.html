TECHNICAL INNOVATION #1

Pre-Submission AI Duplicate Screening

Halting duplicate tickets at the exact moment of creation rather than clogging municipal databases.

How the Screening Protocol Operates

  • 1. Spatial Geofence Scan (50m Radius): When a citizen opens the camera, CivicNexus scans active open tickets within 50 meters of their GPS fix.
  • 2. Perceptual Image Hashing: Uses perceptual image hashes to cross-examine newly captured photos against existing photos in the same micro-area.
  • 3. Semantic NLP Matching: Extracts keywords and intent from voice or text input to ensure high semantic correlation.
  • 4. 1-Click "Support this Issue" Action: If a matching problem exists, the UI prompts the citizen to boost its priority with a single tap rather than creating a duplicate row.

TECHNICAL INNOVATION #2

RepairProof™ & Defect Liability Escrow

Cryptographic and citizen-verified accountability that eliminates ghost closures and holds contractors liable.

Visual Verification

Before/After Slider

Contractors cannot close a work order with text. They must submit high-resolution repair photos cross-aligned with the original incident image.

Anti-Fraud AI

AI Metadata Audit

Validates camera elevation, time stamping, GPS coordinate drift, and pixel tamper forensics to detect stock or fraudulent photo uploads.

Citizen Power

72-Hour Dispute Gate

Tickets enter "Pending Citizen Audit". The complainant has 72 hours to verify the site, confirming "Fixed" or "Disputed" with photographic evidence.

Financial Liability

Defect Escrow (DLP)

Retainage funds (10-15%) are locked during the 12-month Defect Liability Period. Re-emerging defects auto-penalize the contractor.
